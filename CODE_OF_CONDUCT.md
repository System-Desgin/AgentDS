# Code of Conduct

AgentDS is committed to a constructive, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and receive it in good faith.
- Respect upstream maintainers, trademarks, licenses, and attribution.
- Correct mistakes openly, especially when published source claims are wrong.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project
spaces. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and
public conversations made on behalf of AgentDS.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to contact@oday-bakkour.com. Include links or
screenshots when useful. The maintainer will review reports confidentially,
respond proportionately, and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.

Responses may include a private warning, removal of content, a temporary limit
on participation, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against someone who reports a
concern is itself a violation.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove contributions or comments that do not align with
them and will explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe and
appropriate.
